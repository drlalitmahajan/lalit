About Dr. Lalit Mahajan

Dr. Lalit Mahajan is a respected social leader and community representative from Charkhi Dadri, Haryana. Through his active engagement in education, youth development, and social welfare, he has been a guiding force in empowering the Vaish community and fostering unity among diverse groups.

He works tirelessly to promote educational excellence through the Vaish Education Society and continues to inspire youth towards community service and leadership with compassion, integrity, and purpose.

Positions & Leadership Roles

  • General Secretary, Vaish Education Society, Charkhi Dadri
  • Former General Secretary, Agrawal Sabha (Regd.), Charkhi Dadri
  • State Secretary & General Secretary (Youth), Haryana Pradesh Vaish Mahasammelan
  • National Executive Member, Bharatiya Udyog Vyapar Mandal
